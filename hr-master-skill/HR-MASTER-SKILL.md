# HR Master Skill — Knowledge Base
**A comprehensive wiki synthesising CIPD (L3/L5/L7) and SHRM (CP/SCP) certifications, plus expert thought leadership and case studies.**

---

## What This Is

The HR Master Skill is a structured, searchable knowledge base covering **340 HR concepts** across **15 domains**, drawn from:

- **CIPD Foundation Certificate in People Practice (Level 3)** — 4 core units
- **CIPD Associate Diploma in People Management (Level 5)** — 3 core + 3 HR pathway + 1 optional
- **CIPD Advanced Diploma in Strategic People Management (Level 7)** — 4 core + 3 HR specialist + 1 optional
- **SHRM Certified Professional (SHRM-CP)** — operational level
- **SHRM Senior Certified Professional (SHRM-SCP)** — strategic level
- The **2026 SHRM BASK** framework (8 behavioural competencies + 14 functional areas)
- Expert thought leadership: David Ulrich, Josh Bersin, Amy Edmondson, John Kotter, Gallup, McKinsey, Deloitte, WTW, Mercer, Catalyst, and others
- Case studies and applied scenarios for procedural/technical topics

Designed for **all levels** — from HR newcomers seeking foundational definitions to CHROs needing strategic perspective.

## How to Use This Knowledge Base

Each concept entry follows a consistent structure:

1. **Definition** — what it is in 1-2 sentences
2. **Foundational** — accessible explanation for beginners
3. **Practitioner Application** — frameworks, tools, processes
4. **Strategic / Advanced** — CHRO/board-level perspective
5. **Expert Insight** — references to thought leaders, research, frameworks
6. **Case Study / Application** — realistic scenario (especially for List-B concepts)
7. **Key Takeaways** — bullet-point summary

**List-A** concepts (~275) have rich public thought leadership commentary.
**List-B** concepts (~65) are primarily procedural/legal/technical, given case-study treatment.

## Limitations & Disclosures

1. **No automated web-scraped podcast transcripts**: This wiki synthesises expert thought leadership rather than direct podcast transcripts. Sources cited reflect the body of work of named experts.
2. **Snapshot in time**: HR law, regulation, and practice evolve rapidly. Verify currency before applying — especially for jurisdiction-specific legal content (US state pay transparency laws, UK employment law updates, EU directives).
3. **General guidance, not legal advice**: Case studies illustrate principles; specific situations require professional legal/HR counsel.
4. **CIPD/SHRM exam preparation**: This is a comprehensive reference but does not replace official preparation materials. Use as supplement to official CIPD/SHRM course materials.
5. **Authorship**: AI-generated synthesis from extensive training data including HR thought leadership, certification frameworks, and practitioner literature. Reviewed for coherence but not formally peer-reviewed.

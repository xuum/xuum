# SHRM Certifications: Complete Topic & Competency Reference
## SHRM-CP (Certified Professional) and SHRM-SCP (Senior Certified Professional)

Both certifications are built on the same framework — the **SHRM Body of Applied Skills and Knowledge (SHRM BASK)** — and test the same 14 functional areas and 8 behavioral competencies. The difference is the **proficiency level**: SHRM-CP assesses operational-level competency ("For All HR Professionals"), while SHRM-SCP additionally assesses advanced/strategic-level competency ("For Advanced HR Professionals").

---

## EXAM STRUCTURE (Both Certifications)

- 134 multiple-choice questions total (110 scored, 24 unscored field-test items)
- Time limit: 3 hours 40 minutes (two 110-minute sections)
- Question types:
  - 50% Knowledge Items (KIs) — stand-alone questions on the 14 HR functional areas
  - 40% Situational Judgment Items (SJIs) — scenario-based, testing behavioral competency clusters
  - 10% Foundational Knowledge Items (FKIs) — stand-alone questions on behavioral competency key concepts

**SHRM-SCP distinction**: SJIs are evaluated at an advanced proficiency level — candidates must demonstrate enterprise-wide, multi-stakeholder, long-term strategic thinking, not just implementation.

## PART I — BEHAVIORAL COMPETENCIES (8 Competencies in 3 Clusters)

### 2026 BASK Change Note
The 2026 BASK reduced behavioral competencies from 9 to 8 by merging the former **Inclusion & Diversity** and **Global Mindset** competencies into one new competency: **Inclusive Mindset**. AI and data analytics expectations were also expanded across all competencies.

### CLUSTER 1: LEADERSHIP

#### Competency 1: Leadership & Navigation
Sub-competencies: Navigating the Organization; Vision; Managing HR Initiatives; Influence.
Key Concepts: organizational politics/culture, HR as strategic business partner, change leadership vs management, vision communication, stakeholder buy-in, project planning, benchmarking, growth path workforce impact, HR's role in transitions, data-driven adjustments.

#### Competency 2: Ethical Practice
Sub-competencies: Personal Integrity; Professional Integrity; Ethical Agent.
Key Concepts: codes of ethics, confidentiality, conflicts of interest, whistleblower protections, corporate governance, ethical decision frameworks, compliance vs values ethics, HR's dual duty, ethics in HR data/analytics.

#### Competency 3: Inclusive Mindset (NEW in 2026 BASK)
Sub-competencies: Fostering Inclusion; Cultural Agility; Equity and Belonging; Neurodiversity and Accessibility; Global HR Strategy.
Key Concepts: DEIB frameworks, psychological safety, unconscious bias, pay equity/transparency, intersectionality, cross-cultural communication, neurodiversity, digital accessibility, skills-first hiring, ERGs, measuring inclusion, global nondiscrimination, inclusive leadership.

### CLUSTER 2: INTERPERSONAL

#### Competency 4: Relationship Management
Sub-competencies: Networking; Relationship Building; Teamwork; Negotiation; Conflict Management.
Key Concepts: credibility/trust, internal customer service, coalition building, principled negotiation, conflict resolution models, mediation, difficult conversations, cross-functional collaboration.

#### Competency 5: Communication
Sub-competencies: Verbal/Written Communication; Feedback; Facilitation; Active Listening; Persuasion; Diplomacy.
Key Concepts: audience adaptation, translating HR data for business audiences, crisis communication, announcement strategy, non-verbal cues, intercultural communication, executive briefings.

### CLUSTER 3: BUSINESS

#### Competency 6: Business Acumen
Sub-competencies: Business and Competitive Awareness; Business Analysis; Strategic Alignment.
Key Concepts: financial statement literacy, ROI of HR programs, business model, industry/competitive analysis, SWOT/PESTEL, balanced scorecard, HR value proposition, KPIs, labor market dynamics.

#### Competency 7: Consultation
Sub-competencies: Evaluating Business Challenges; Designing HR Solutions; Advising on HR Solutions; Change Management; Service Excellence.
Key Concepts: needs assessment, gap analysis, change models (Kotter, ADKAR, Lewin), project management, stakeholder analysis, business cases, program evaluation, SLAs, resistance management, pilot design.

#### Competency 8: Analytical Aptitude
Sub-competencies: Data Advocate; Data Gathering; Data Analysis; Evidence-Based Decision-Making.
Key Concepts: HR metrics/KPIs (turnover, time-to-fill, CPH, engagement, absenteeism), people analytics, predictive analytics, quant vs qual methods, survey design, benchmarking, correlation vs causation, regression, data viz, dashboards, data privacy, HRIS, ROI, AI/ML in HR analytics.

## PART II — HR EXPERTISE (14 Functional Areas in 3 Domains)

### DOMAIN A: PEOPLE

#### FA1: HR Strategy
- Strategic planning process and models (SWOT, PESTEL, Porter's Five Forces)
- Mission, vision, values development
- HR as strategic business partner (Ulrich Model and evolution)
- HR value proposition and ROI
- Workforce planning at strategic level
- Make/buy/borrow workforce decisions
- HR metrics and measurement (HR scorecard, Balanced Scorecard)
- M&A, divestitures, restructurings HR role
- HR governance, policies, ethics in strategic decisions
- SHRM-SCP: Enterprise HR strategy, board influence, risk management

#### FA2: Talent Acquisition
- Job analysis and design (O*NET, competency-based profiles, job evaluation, skills-based requirements)
- Sourcing/recruiting (active vs passive, employer branding, EVP, social media recruiting, referrals, campus recruiting, diversity sourcing, pipelines, funnel metrics)
- Selection (employment tests cognitive/personality/skill/work sample, structured/unstructured/behavioral/STAR/situational/panel interviews, assessment centers, validity/reliability, adverse impact, background checks, FCRA, drug testing)
- Offers and negotiations
- Strategic onboarding (pre-boarding, orientation, 30/60/90 plans, measurement, technology)
- Retention (stay interviews, early turnover identification)
- Legal compliance (EEO, AAPs, I-9, recordkeeping)
- Technology (ATS, AI in recruiting, video interviewing)
- SHRM-SCP: Enterprise TA strategy, employer brand strategy, recruiting ROI

#### FA3: Employee Engagement & Retention
- Engagement fundamentals (vs satisfaction vs commitment, Gallup Q12, psychological contract, intrinsic/extrinsic motivation, EX vs engagement)
- Measuring engagement (surveys, pulse, benchmarks, turnover/absenteeism, eNPS)
- Retention strategies (turnover analysis, cost of turnover, stay/exit interviews, predictive analytics, career pathing, manager quality, flexible work, recognition)
- Organizational culture (definition, assessment, change, values alignment, psychological safety)
- Manager-employee relationship (effectiveness, training, 1-on-1s, coaching)
- Recognition programs (monetary/non-monetary, peer, formal/informal)
- Work-life integration (flexible work, wellbeing, burnout prevention)
- SHRM-SCP: Enterprise engagement strategy, engagement-business outcomes link, culture transformation

#### FA4: Learning & Development
- Needs assessment (TNA, organizational/task/person analysis, gap analysis, business alignment)
- Instructional design (ADDIE, SAM, Bloom's Taxonomy, andragogy/Knowles, learning styles, 70-20-10, microlearning, e-learning, blended)
- Delivery methods (ILT, e-learning, LMS, OJT, mentoring, coaching, action learning, stretch assignments, shadowing, rotation, communities of practice, simulations, gamification)
- Career development (career pathing/ladders/lattices, IDPs, succession planning, HiPos, leadership development, mentoring)
- Learning evaluation (Kirkpatrick 4 levels, Phillips ROI 5th level, learning transfer, LMS analytics)
- Organizational learning (knowledge management, communities of practice, learning organization/Senge, continuous learning culture)
- Compliance/mandatory training
- SHRM-SCP: Enterprise learning strategy, learning culture at scale, L&D ROI, learning tech strategy (LMS/LXP/AI)

#### FA5: Total Rewards
- Compensation fundamentals (total rewards philosophy, internal/external equity, job evaluation methods, pay structures, market pricing, salary surveys, geographic differentials, pay compression)
- Base pay administration (merit pay, P4P, salary ranges, compa-ratio, pay equity, pay transparency)
- Variable pay/incentives (STIs: bonuses/commissions/profit-sharing/gainsharing; LTIs: stock options/RSUs/deferred comp; sales comp; executive comp; team vs individual)
- Benefits (health/welfare, ACA, COBRA, Section 125 cafeteria plans, EAPs, voluntary benefits)
- Retirement (qualified vs nonqualified, DB, DC/401(k)/403(b)/457, ERISA, fiduciary, vesting, profit-sharing)
- Leave/time-off (PTO, parental leave, FMLA intersection, STD/LTD)
- Executive/specialized comp (CEO pay ratio, say-on-pay, deferred comp, perquisites)
- Pay equity/legal (Equal Pay Act, pay equity audits, FLSA exempt/non-exempt, minimum wage, overtime)
- Total rewards communication
- SHRM-SCP: Enterprise total rewards strategy, executive comp governance, pay equity strategic/legal risk

### DOMAIN B: ORGANIZATION

#### FA6: Structure of the HR Function
- HR delivery models (centralized, decentralized, shared services, CoEs, HRBP, hybrid/matrix, outsourced/PEOs, Ulrich Three-Legged Stool)
- HR operating model design (matching structure to org strategy, HR transformation, insourcing/outsourcing, vendor management)
- HR governance (policies/procedures, compliance oversight, risk management role, HR audits, operational metrics)
- HR's roles (Ulrich: strategic partner, administrative, employee advocate, change agent; SME; generalist vs specialist)
- HR effectiveness measurement (HR-to-employee ratio, cost-per-transaction, satisfaction, SLAs, continuous improvement)
- HR staffing and professional development
- SHRM-SCP: HR transformation leadership, enterprise operating model, business case for HR change

#### FA7: Organizational Effectiveness & Development
- OD fundamentals (vs change management, systems thinking, OD process model, action research, Appreciative Inquiry)
- Diagnosis/assessment (assessment tools, culture assessment, 360s, strengths-based)
- Change management (Kotter, Lewin, ADKAR, McKinsey 7S; readiness; resistance; communication; leadership; sustaining change)
- OD interventions (process consultation, team building, large-group interventions, structural, human process, strategic)
- Performance management (cycle, SMART goals, appraisal methods, continuous PM, calibration, PIPs, comp linkage, coaching, underperformers, progressive discipline)
- Organizational design (structures: functional/divisional/matrix/flat/network; span of control; job design: enrichment/enlargement/Hackman-Oldham; redesign)
- Succession planning and talent management (methodology, 9-box, HiPo, critical roles, talent reviews)
- SHRM-SCP: Enterprise OD strategy, culture transformation, enterprise redesign, future capability, OD metrics

#### FA8: Workforce Management
- Workforce planning (SWP process, demand forecasting, supply analysis, gap analysis, planning horizons, scenarios)
- Workforce analytics (data sources, headcount/turnover/vacancy/time-to-fill metrics, predictive analytics, skill gap analysis, dashboards)
- Succession planning (process, bench strength, 9-box, critical positions, emergency succession, governance)
- Staffing models/flexibility (FT/PT/flex strategies, contingent workforce, outsourcing, redeployment, rightsizing, layoffs/WARN)
- Skills/competency management (frameworks, inventories, reskilling/upskilling, future skills)
- Immigration/work authorization (H-1B, L-1, O-1, TN; I-9; E-Verify)
- SHRM-SCP: Enterprise workforce planning as business strategy, M&A workforce, board reporting, flexible workforce strategies

#### FA9: Employee & Labor Relations
- Employee relations (relationship types, psychological contract, employee handbook, policy interpretation, conduct expectations, grievance procedures, open-door)
- Performance/discipline (PIPs, progressive discipline, documentation, coaching vs discipline, terminations, wrongful termination risk, separation agreements)
- Workplace investigations (process, neutrality, harassment/discrimination, workplace violence, whistleblower, legal considerations)
- Labor relations/union (US labor history, NLRA, NLRB, Section 7 rights, ULPs, organizing/campaign, elections, certification/decertification, collective bargaining, mandatory/permissive/illegal subjects, contract administration, grievance arbitration, strikes/lockouts/picketing, duty to bargain in good faith)
- Employee communications (internal comms, transparency, sensitive comms)
- Conflict resolution (ADR, mediation, arbitration, peer review, ombudsman)
- SHRM-SCP: Enterprise labor strategy, union avoidance, complex negotiations, ER risk advisory

#### FA10: Technology Management
- HRIS (core HR, payroll, talent management, analytics; selection/implementation/change; ERP modules SAP/Oracle/Workday; data integrity; integrations)
- HR tech landscape (ATS, LMS/LXP, PM platforms, recognition/engagement, comp tools, benefits admin, payroll)
- AI in HR (TA applications, PM/analytics, bias risks, ethical use, regulations, generative AI, transparency)
- People analytics tech (dashboards, predictive platforms, planning software, data lakes)
- Data privacy/security (employee data protection, GDPR HR implications, security practices, consent, state laws CCPA)
- Workplace technology policies (acceptable use, social media, monitoring, BYOD, remote tech)
- Emerging tech (blockchain credentials, chatbots, AR/VR L&D)
- SHRM-SCP: HR tech strategy/roadmap, governance/ethics oversight, enterprise analytics capability

### DOMAIN C: WORKPLACE

#### FA11: Managing a Global Workforce
- Global HR strategy (standardization vs localization, multinationals, operating models, cultural intelligence/CQ)
- Cross-cultural competence (Hofstede, Trompenaars, GLOBE; country-specific norms; cross-cultural teams; ethnocentrism avoidance; communication)
- Expatriate management (selection, pre-departure prep, host-country orientation, compensation/balance sheet, repatriation, TCNs, assignment failure)
- International comp/benefits (principles, COL adjustments, tax equalization, host-country benefits, global mobility)
- International employment law (ILO standards, country variations, works councils EU, GDPR, immigration)
- Global talent management (TA strategies, local vs global, global leadership development, pipelines)
- SHRM-SCP: Global HR strategy governance, geopolitical risk, global organizational design

#### FA12: Risk Management
- Workplace safety/health (OSHA, General Duty Clause, hazard ID, SMS, incident investigation/reporting, OSHA recordkeeping 300/301/300A, workers' comp, return-to-work, wellness)
- Workplace security (violence prevention, threat assessment, security protocols, emergency response)
- Crisis management/BCP (BCP, disaster recovery, emergency response, pandemic prep, crisis communication)
- HR risk identification (EPL risks, harassment/discrimination liability, wage-hour, benefits, retaliation, HR audits)
- Employee privacy/data security (monitoring rights, ADA/GINA medical confidentiality, breach response)
- Drug/alcohol programs (DFW policies, drug testing types, DOT regulations, medical marijuana state implications)
- SHRM-SCP: ERM and HR's role, board risk reporting, organizational resilience, strategic workforce risk

#### FA13: Corporate Social Responsibility
- CSR foundations (definition, frameworks, history; CSR vs ESG; stakeholder theory/Freeman; Triple Bottom Line; Carroll's Pyramid)
- Environmental responsibility (sustainability practices, green HR, carbon footprint, ESG reporting HR role)
- Social responsibility (community engagement, philanthropy, employee volunteer programs, supply chain ethics, human rights, forced/child labor prevention)
- Governance/ethics (corporate governance, ethics programs/codes, whistleblower, anti-corruption/bribery, board diversity)
- HR's role in CSR (embedding in HR practices, CSR as engagement/retention, GRI/UN SDG reporting, employee comms, wellbeing as CSR)
- Sustainability/future of work (sustainable workforce, long-term talent thinking, responsible restructuring)
- SHRM-SCP: CSR/ESG strategy/governance, leadership advisory, enterprise integration, stakeholder reporting

#### FA14: U.S. Employment Law & Regulations
- EEO Laws: Title VII, CRA 1991, ADEA, ADA, ADAAA, PDA, PWFA, PUMP Act, GINA, Equal Pay Act, Lilly Ledbetter, EO 11246, Rehabilitation Act
- Wage and Hour: FLSA, exempt/non-exempt, overtime, recordkeeping, state laws, pay stubs, payment timing
- Leave Laws: FMLA, ADA/FMLA/WC interaction, state leave, paid sick leave, USERRA
- Labor Relations: NLRA, Taft-Hartley, Landrum-Griffin
- Benefits Laws: ERISA, COBRA, HIPAA, ACA, MHPAEA
- Safety: OSH Act, workers' comp state frameworks
- Privacy/Data: ECPA, FCRA, state privacy (CCPA)
- Immigration: IRCA, I-9, visa categories, E-Verify
- Anti-Harassment/Retaliation: sexual harassment (quid pro quo, hostile work environment), EEOC guidance, retaliation protections
- Contracts: at-will doctrine and exceptions, express/implied contracts, non-competes, NDAs, arbitration agreements
- Compliance: EEOC charge process, OFCCP Affirmative Action, DOL audits, state-federal conflicts, emerging areas (pay transparency, biometric laws, AI in hiring laws)
- SHRM-SCP: C-suite/board legal risk advisory, proactive compliance programs, EEOC/litigation strategy, legislative monitoring

## SHRM-CP vs SHRM-SCP

| Dimension | SHRM-CP | SHRM-SCP |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Primary Focus | Operational implementation | Strategic leadership and design |
| Proficiency | All HR Professionals | All + Advanced HR Professionals |
| Situational Judgment | Departmental/team-level | Enterprise/executive-level |
| Typical Work | Policy admin, program implementation | Policy creation, HR governance, executive advising |
| Eligibility | 1-4 years HR experience | 3+ years strategic/senior HR |

## 2026 BASK Changes
- Behavioral competencies: 9 → 8 (Inclusion & Diversity + Global Mindset merged into Inclusive Mindset)
- Inclusive Mindset expanded: neurodiversity, accessibility, psychological safety, pay transparency
- AI/tech expectations expanded across all competencies
- Data analytics emphasis increased

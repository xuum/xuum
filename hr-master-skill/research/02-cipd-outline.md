# CIPD HR Certifications — Complete Syllabus Reference (2025/2026)

## LEVEL 3: CIPD Foundation Certificate in People Practice
4 core units, written assignments, Foundation CIPD membership, A-level equivalent.

### 3CO01 — Business, Culture and Change in Context
**LO1 Business environment:** nature of organisations/people profession; PESTLE (political, economic, social, technological, legal, environmental); competitive environment; technology in HR (internet, email, HR systems, collaboration); organisation types (private, public, third sector); organisational goals.
**LO2 Behaviour & culture:** culture definition & formation; organisation as a system; behaviour shaping culture; culture-performance link; leadership/management style impact; Handy's cultures (Power, Role, Task, Person); human capital theory; learning-performance link; EDI as cultural factor; psychological safety.
**LO3 Change management:** drivers of change; impact on people; people professionals' role; intro change models; stakeholder management; communication; resistance to change.

### 3CO02 — Principles of Analytics
**LO1 EBP:** evidence-based practice in HR; data types (quantitative, qualitative, primary, secondary); measurement methods; calculations (headcount, turnover, absence, cost-per-hire, ROI basics); financial info interpretation; reporting formats; policy/procedure in decisions; data risks/limitations; HR tech.
**LO2 Value creation:** organisational value concept; value for customers/employees/stakeholders; stakeholder theory; people practice-performance link; analytics for working practice; data ethics; GDPR basics.

### 3CO03 — Core Behaviours for People Professionals
**LO1 Ethics:** CIPD Profession Map; core behaviours (ethical practice, valuing people, passion for learning, commercial drive, insights-focused, situational decisions, courage, inclusivity); ethical principles (stewardship, integrity, competence); compliance; ethical dilemmas; customer focus.
**LO2 Respect & inclusion:** inclusion/diversity; Equality Act 2010 protected characteristics (age, disability, gender reassignment, marriage/CP, pregnancy/maternity, race, religion/belief, sex, sexual orientation); inclusive practices; challenging inappropriate behaviour; positive relationships; wellbeing; micro-aggressions/unconscious bias.
**LO3 Reflective practice:** reflective practice (Gibbs, Kolb intro); CPD; knowledge/skills gaps; feedback; lifelong learning; professional courage.

### 3CO04 — Essentials of People Practice
**LO1 Employee lifecycle:** stages (attraction/employer branding, R&S, onboarding, development, retention/engagement, separation); HR role at each; HR policies; psychological contract.
**LO2 Legislation & relationships:** Equality Act 2010, Employment Rights Act 1996, Health & Safety at Work Act 1974, Working Time Regs 1998, Minimum Wage, GDPR; contract types (permanent, fixed-term, zero-hours, agency); policies & procedures; grievance/discipline; rights & duties; Employment Tribunal.
**LO3 R&S:** job analysis; JDs; person specs; recruitment methods (internal/external; advantages/disadvantages); selection methods (applications, shortlisting, interview types, psychometrics, assessment centres); selection interview; appointment decisions; D&I in recruitment; pre-employment checks (refs, RTW, DBS); offer & comms.
**LO4 Performance management:** purpose; SMART objectives; monitoring/reviewing; constructive feedback; appraisals; underperformance/capability; PM-reward link; documentation.
**LO5 L&D:** importance; TNA; methods (OJT, coaching, mentoring, e-learning, classroom, blended); designing/delivering activity; Kirkpatrick intro.
**LO6 Reward:** purpose; intrinsic vs extrinsic; total reward; pay structures intro; NMW/NLW; designing approach; motivation theories (Maslow, Herzberg intro); pay equity & equal pay.

## LEVEL 5: CIPD Associate Diploma in People Management
3 core + 3 HR pathway + 1 optional unit; written assignments; Assoc CIPD; undergraduate degree level.

### 5CO01 — Organisational Performance and Culture in Practice
**LO1 Structure/strategy/environment:** structures (hierarchical, flat, matrix, network/virtual, divisional, functional, holacratic); strategy models (Porter, Ansoff, customer value chain, HR-business alignment); external factors (PESTLE in depth, labour market trends, globalisation, regulation, geopolitical); technology (digital transformation, automation, AI, HRIS, gig economy, hybrid/remote).
**LO2 Behaviour theories & culture:** SCARF (Rock); Handy; Schein (artefacts, espoused values, basic assumptions); Hofstede; Lewin Force Field Analysis; McKinsey 7S; McGregor X/Y; people practices-culture impact; change approaches (Lewin, Kotter, ADKAR, Prosci); change experience models (Kübler-Ross, Bridges); wellbeing (physical/mental/financial/social, CIPD framework, engagement link, stress/psychosocial).
**LO3 People practice-goals link:** lifecycle-HR role link; HR interconnections; internal consulting (stakeholder mapping, business partnering, SLAs).

### 5CO02 — Evidence-Based Practice
**LO1 Critical thinking & decisions:** EBP (scientific research, organisational data, stakeholder input, professional experience); analysis tools (SWOT, PESTLE, Porter Five Forces, McKinsey 7S, Balanced Scorecard, Fishbone, Force Field, Ansoff, target operating model); critical thinking (objective reasoning, fact vs assumption, bias awareness, logical fallacies).
**LO2 Decision-making:** data interpretation (statistical, thematic, people analytics, absence/turnover/engagement data); reporting (business case, audience-specific presentation, visualisation).
**LO3 Ethics in decisions:** ethical perspectives (utilitarianism, deontology, communitarianism, virtue ethics); ethical dilemmas; values-based decisions; impact on individuals/orgs/society. **Decision models:** rational, bounded rationality (Simon), individual vs group, groupthink/polarisation, heuristics, cognitive biases.

### 5CO03 — Professional Behaviours and Valuing People
**LO1 People professional identity:** Profession Map; inclusivity, courage, ethics; personal/professional values; integrity, honesty, fairness; professional courage; ethical dilemmas.
**LO2 Evolving role:** transactional → strategic HR; Ulrich three-legged stool; tech impact; future trends.
**LO3 Inclusion benefits:** inclusion/belonging theories (Maslow, Self-Determination Theory/Ryan-Deci, Social Identity Theory); inclusive initiatives; business case for D&I; psychological safety.
**LO4 CPD planning:** self-assessment; 360 feedback; formal/informal development (coaching, mentoring, training, peer learning, reading); PDP; CIPD CPD framework.

### 5HR01 — Employment Relationship Management
**LO1 Voice & engagement:** psychological contract (transactional/relational, breach/violation); involvement vs participation; voice tools (surveys, suggestion schemes, briefings, JCCs, works councils, unions, digital channels); engagement-performance link; emerging trends.
**LO2 Conflict:** organisational conflict vs misbehaviour; sanctions (strikes, work-to-rule, go-slow); grievance/discipline skills (investigation, hearings, natural justice, right of accompaniment); conciliation/mediation/arbitration (ACAS, CAC).
**LO3 Performance/capability/conduct:** PM as ER tool; capability vs conduct; discipline stages; grievance stages; sickness absence.
**LO4 Collective law/unions:** TULRCA 1992; right to organise; statutory recognition (CAC); information & consultation; redundancy consultation; rep types (recognised unions, works councils, staff forums, EWCs); collective bargaining (single-table, multi-employer, pattern, wage-setting).

### 5HR02 — Talent Management and Workforce Planning
**LO1 Labour market:** trends (skill shortages, demographics, gig, migration, automation); strategic positioning (employer brand, EVP, employer of choice).
**LO2 Workforce planning:** hard/soft WFP; scenario planning; techniques (demand forecasting, supply analysis, gap analysis, action planning); succession/contingency (frameworks, 9-box, key person risk, BCP); R&S methods (job boards, social, referrals, agencies, direct sourcing, ACs; validity/reliability; structured vs unstructured; work samples, psychometrics, personality).
**LO3 Talent management:** turnover/retention (calculation, push/pull, exit interviews, cost); developing/retaining (career pathways, HiPos, talent pools, mentoring/coaching, stretch assignments, secondments); diverse talent pools (inclusive practices, pipeline diversity, blind recruitment, apprenticeships, returners).
**LO4 Contracts & onboarding:** contract types (permanent FT/PT, fixed-term, zero-hours, casual/seasonal, agency, freelancers/IR35); terms (express/implied, custom/practice, statute, collective agreement, restrictive covenants); onboarding (pre-boarding, induction, cultural integration, role clarity, buddy/mentor, probationary review).

### 5HR03 — Reward for Performance and Contribution
**LO1 Reward impact:** principles (intrinsic, extrinsic, psychological link); motivation theories (Maslow, Herzberg two-factor, Vroom expectancy, Adams equity); package elements (base pay, variable, benefits, non-financial, total reward); internal factors (affordability, budgets, values, PM); external factors (labour market, economy, inflation, NLW, sector norms, benchmark data).
**LO2 Reward components:** structures (spot rates, ranges/bands, broad-banding, pay spines, job evaluation analytical/non-analytical); PM-pay link (IPRP, team incentives, profit/gain-sharing, appraisal-linked progression); benefits (statutory/contractual, cafeteria/flexible, pension auto-enrolment).
**LO3 Benchmarking:** pay surveys; market positioning (lead/match/lag); quartiles; pay review processes; equal pay (like work, work rated equivalent, equal value); gender pay gap reporting; pay transparency; ethnicity pay gap voluntary reporting.

### Optional Units (Level 5)
- **5OS01 Specialist Employment Law:** employment regulation aims; ET system; employment status (employee/worker/self-employed); discrimination law (direct/indirect/harassment/victimisation/reasonable adjustments); protected characteristics; varying contracts; redundancy (qualification, consultation, selection, statutory pay); TUPE; contract law; wrongful vs unfair dismissal.
- **5OS02 Advances in Digital L&D:** digital learning content (e-learning, m-learning, video, podcasts, gamification); games-based/simulations; social learning; live online platforms (Zoom, Teams); blended learning; digital badges/micro-credentials; LMS/LXP; risks/challenges; AI in L&D.
- **5OS03 L&D Essentials:** L&D-business link; LNA/TNA; learning theories (behaviourism, cognitivism, constructivism, experiential/Kolb); blended design; Kirkpatrick 4 levels; transfer of learning; coaching/mentoring; alternative methods (peer learning, action learning sets, shadowing); 70:20:10.
- **5OS04 People Management in International Context:** globalisation effects; IHRM approaches (ethnocentric, polycentric, geocentric, regiocentric); expatriates/international assignments; cross-cultural management; diversity across contexts; legal/ethical international challenges; jurisdiction differences; international comp & benefits.
- **5OS05 Diversity and Inclusion:** diversity dimensions (visible/non-visible); business/legal/moral cases; UK equality law; gender/ethnicity pay gap reporting; disability inclusion/reasonable adjustments; intersectionality; org strategies; unconscious bias/stereotype threat; leadership for fairness; monitoring/reporting; impact on satisfaction; inclusive recruitment.
- **5OS06 Leadership and Management Development:** leadership theories (trait, behavioural, situational, transformational/transactional); leadership vs management; L&D theories applied; programme design; 360 feedback; coaching/mentoring for leaders; assessment/development centres; action learning; evaluation.
- **5OS07 Wellbeing at Work:** wellbeing dimensions (physical/mental/financial/social/purposeful); JD-R model; effort-reward imbalance; wellbeing-performance link; CIPD framework; stakeholders (HR, managers, OH, EAP); programme design; mental health (stress, burnout, presenteeism, absenteeism); line manager role; H&S legal obligations.

## LEVEL 7: CIPD Advanced Diploma in Strategic People Management
4 core + 3 HR specialist + 1 optional unit; postgraduate level; Chartered MCIPD.

### 7CO01 — Work and Working Lives in a Changing Business Environment
**LO1 Long-term environment:** globalisation (supply chains, offshoring, MNCs, reshoring/deglobalisation); technology (automation/AI/robotics, future of work WEF/OECD, human-machine collaboration, algorithmic management/surveillance, platform/gig economy); social/demographic (ageing, generations, migration, urbanisation, gender equality).
**LO2 Short-term trends:** public policy impact; political/economic trends; labour market fluctuations; flexible/remote working.
**LO3 Change, innovation, creativity:** leadership for change/innovation; people professionals' role; agile working/organisational agility.
**LO4 Ethics/sustainability/diversity/wellbeing:** strategic wellbeing frameworks; CSR/sustainability theory (Carroll's pyramid, ESG, UN SDGs, green HR, ethical supply chains, B-Corp); interrelationships.

### 7CO02 — People Management & Development Strategies for Performance
**LO1 Alignment:** vertical/horizontal alignment; best fit vs best practice; RBV applied to HR; HPWS.
**LO2 Performance & EX:** people practices-performance evidence; EX across lifecycle; EX as strategic priority; people analytics for evidence.
**LO3 Current practice across HR sub-domains:** resourcing/talent; PM (continuous, OKRs, coaching, post-annual-appraisal); L&D (organisational learning, learning organisation); OD&D; reward (strategic, total reward, exec pay, transparency); ER; engagement (Macleod, Q12); EDI (intersectionality, inclusive leadership, data-driven).
**LO4 People professional influence:** strategic partner; HR influence on decisions; Ulrich evolution; value creators; sector contexts (SME, public, MNC).

### 7CO03 — Personal Effectiveness, Ethics and Business Acumen
**LO1 Inclusivity & ethics:** ethical perspectives applied (utilitarian, deontological, virtue); ethical leadership; moral courage; meaningful voice; personal integrity self-evaluation; championing D&I.
**LO2 Business outcomes:** self-awareness/self-management; business acumen (P&L, financial statements, cost-benefit); commercial awareness; resilience; collaborative working; networking (strategic, CIPD/IES/SHRM, influencing without authority).
**LO3 Personal effectiveness:** ongoing development; advanced reflection (critical incident analysis, action learning, peer supervision); personal dev strategy; impact evaluation.
**LO4 Influence & courage:** stakeholder analysis at board level; political acumen; persuasion/negotiation; executive presence/boardroom comms; courage and challenge; coaching/facilitating; senior conflict management.

### 7CO04 — Business Research in People Practice
**LO1 Planning:** business problem identification; aims/SMART objectives; literature review (academic journals, CIPD, government, think tanks; credibility evaluation; synthesis); research questions.
**LO2 Methods:** primary vs secondary; qualitative/quantitative/mixed; ontology/epistemology (positivism/interpretivism); sampling (probability/non-probability); ethics (consent, anonymity, GDPR, power/bias); limitations.
**LO3 Analysis:** primary data collection; quantitative (descriptive stats, intro regression); qualitative (thematic, content, grounded theory elements); interpretation; triangulation.
**LO4 Recommendations:** conclusions; evidence-based recommendations; cost-benefit; business case report; implementation plan (phasing, resources, KPIs, risk).

### 7HR01 — Strategic Employment Relations
**LO1 Frames of reference:** unitarist/pluralist/radical-Marxist; cooperation vs conflict; employer strategies (derecognition, partnership, substitution, suppression); strategic psychological contract.
**LO2 External institutions:** government (legislation, gig regulation); ACAS/CAC/ET; employer associations & unions; international bodies (ILO, EU social policy, ECHR).
**LO3 Strategic voice:** collective bargaining (single/multi-employer, decentralisation, legal status); arbitration/conciliation/mediation; enterprise-wide engagement; ICE Regulations 2004.
**LO4 ER risk:** industrial action risk; collective disputes; strike/picketing legal framework; proactive ER risk strategies.

### 7HR02 — Resourcing and Talent Management to Sustain Success
**LO1 Changing environment:** tight markets; digital/AI in recruiting (ATS, AI screening, video); gig/portfolio careers; DEI hiring imperatives; post-pandemic shifts; regulation (IR35, agency work).
**LO2 Strategic R&S:** build vs buy; internal talent markets; employer brand/EVP strategic; selection validity/reliability (structured behavioural/STAR, cognitive ability, SJTs, ACs, work samples, AI video ethics); structural bias reduction.
**LO3 Succession & retention:** 9-box; talent pools; succession depth; critical role identification; retention drivers; programmes (flex working, career dev, recognition); stay interviews; brand ambassadorship.
**LO4 Performance & exits:** technology for performance monitoring (productivity data, recording, surveillance, remote tools); surveillance ethics/algorithmic management; attendance & underperformance; retirement/redundancy/dismissal (legal, ethical, fair process).

### 7HR03 — Strategic Reward Management
**LO1 Strategy:** reward-business strategy/culture link; frameworks (Armstrong & Brown total reward); financial (base, variable, bonuses, LTIPs); non-financial (recognition, dev, flex, purpose); benefits (health, pensions, perks, family-friendly).
**LO2 Pay structures:** job evaluation (analytical: factor comparison/points rating; non-analytical: ranking/paired); structures (graded, broad-banding, spines, incremental); market pricing/benchmarking; incremental scale critique; contingency pay (IPRP, competency-based, skills-based, team bonuses, profit-sharing, LTIPs/share schemes, sales commission); cafeteria vs universal benefits; ethics of contingency reward.
**LO3 Legal/ethical:** equal pay & job evaluation; gender pay gap (causes, reporting, action); living wage vs minimum wage; pay transparency; exec pay scrutiny/shareholder oversight; pay ratio (FTSE 350); ethnicity/disability pay gaps; collective bargaining in pay.

### Optional Units (Level 7)
- **7OS01 Advanced Employment Law in Practice:** sources (statute, common law, EU-retained, ILO); precedent; vicarious liability; continuity; ET system (jurisdiction, procedure, ACAS Early Conciliation, EAT/CoA/SC appeals); employment status legal tests (mutuality, control, personal service, integration); gig economy cases; Equality Act 2010 in depth (all 9 PCs; direct/indirect with justification; harassment incl third-party; victimisation; discrimination arising from disability; reasonable adjustments); positive action vs positive discrimination; defences; remedies (uncapped, declaration, recommendation); burden of proof; contract formation; implied terms; varying T&Cs; termination (dismissal types, wrongful/unfair, fair reasons CCRSS, band of reasonable responses, constructive dismissal, redundancy, TUPE/ETO defence); H&S law; Working Time Regs; NMW/NLW; family-friendly (maternity/paternity/adoption/SPL/parental/bereavement/flexible working day-one); GDPR/employee monitoring.
- **7OS02 Learning and Development Practice:** external environment; L&D-strategy link; LNA at three levels; designing/delivering/measuring interventions; evaluation (Kirkpatrick, Phillips ROI, Anderson value); org learning theory (Argyris & Schön single/double-loop, Senge); communities of practice (Wenger); KM (explicit/tacit); commissioning complex L&D; coaching/mentoring strategic; managing L&D function/budget; strategic impact measurement.
- **7OS03 Technology Enhanced Learning:** TEL types (e-learning, virtual classrooms, m-learning, gamification, AR/VR, social learning); LMS vs LXP; learning analytics; AI in L&D (adaptive, chatbots, tutors); implementation (governance, procurement, change management); adoption barriers; blending; effectiveness evaluation; ethics (privacy, accessibility, digital equity).
- **7OS04 Advanced Equality, Diversity and Inclusion:** theoretical frameworks (Tajfel/Turner Social Identity, Crenshaw intersectionality, critical race theory, feminist perspectives); UK labour market data (occupational segregation horizontal/vertical, pay gaps, participation); advanced legal/moral/business cases; beyond compliance → genuine inclusion; visible/non-visible diversity; inclusive leadership; strategic EDI embedding; communication/training; addressing discrimination/harassment/micro-aggressions; unions & line managers role; metrics & reporting; compound disadvantage.
- **7OS05 Managing People in an International Context:** international expansion modes (export, JV, M&A, greenfield, franchise); international structures (global, transnational, multinational, international); varieties of capitalism (CME vs LME); institutional arrangements; legal systems (US, EU, China, India, ME); expatriate management (selection, prep, cross-cultural training, comp balance sheet/host/home, repatriation); cross-cultural (Hofstede, GLOBE, Trompenaars & Hampden-Turner); ethics (labour standards, supply chains, human rights/ILO, anti-bribery, modern slavery); performance across cultures; international team diversity; global flexible working.
- **7OS06 Wellbeing at Work (Advanced):** multi-dimensional wellbeing models; theoretical frameworks (JD-R/Bakker-Demerouti, ERI/Siegrist, Conservation of Resources/Hobfoll, Self-Determination, PERMA/Seligman); critical significance for employers/employees; social responsibilities; contextual influences (macro, organisational, individual); strategies (mental health first aid, EAPs, OH, physical health, financial wellbeing, flex working); integration with people management; measuring (surveys, absence, EAP utilisation, productivity); line manager role; wellbeing during change; cost-benefit; legal framework (duty of care, stress risk assessments).
- **7LD01 Organisational Design and Development:** OD theories (contingency, systems, sociotechnical); design options (hierarchical, flat, matrix, integrated, network/virtual, holacracy); structure-culture link; OD discipline (humanistic/democratic/developmental values); interventions (large group, appreciative inquiry, team development, culture change, process consultation); action research foundation; change impact on forms; iterative OD cycles (diagnosis/design/intervention/evaluation); implementation (systematic transition, stakeholder engagement, communication, resistance); people professional skills (facilitation, systemic thinking, change expertise, coaching); evaluating OD effectiveness.
